EYE OF BELL - playable demo (Windows 64-bit)

Run "Eye of Bell.exe". Keep "Eye of Bell.pck" in the same folder. No install needed.
Starts in borderless fullscreen; Alt+Enter switches to a window.

Goal: find your way through the dungeon to the stairs down (one of the keys opens the locked
door in front of them). Levers, a pressure plate and a cracked wall (War Hammer or Fireball) help.

NEW IN v0.7
  The dark is real now: carry a torch or a candle, and watch it burn down. Right-click an item on
  the floor to choose who carries it. A bear trap can be dropped in front to set it, and nothing
  gets past one. Details below.

NEW IN v0.6
  Hold Monster (the Mage's new spell), a rusty bear trap to set, monsters that move at their own
  slower pace, a party that talks, and you see what hit you.

THE MONSTERS
  Skeletons, skeleton guards, slimes and acid slimes all have detailed art with full animation:
  they idle, walk, attack (the acid slime spits) and react when hit.
  They move on their own, slower clock, pause a moment before following, and can't strike the
  moment they arrive. Step back and you get a breather or a free swing; keep walking and you
  leave them behind. Only one at a time tries to get round to your side or back; the rest queue.
  When a blow lands you see what hit you: claw rakes, an axe swing, a splat of slime.

MOVING AND ACTING
  W / S          forward / back          A / D   sidestep        Q / E   turn
  (or the six on-screen arrows)
  F              use a door, lever, or the cracked wall in front of you
  Left click     pick up / put down an item on the tile in front
               (a key goes straight to a backpack: "Key picked up!")
  Shift+click    send a floor item straight to a quick slot or backpack
  Right-click    on a floor item: choose which hero carries it (see LOOT below)

THE PARTY
  Click a hero's hand button to attack, throw, or cast (see below)
  1 - 8          the quick slots under the portraits (potions, scrolls, darts, daggers, traps,
                 torches and candles)
  Click a portrait or name   open that hero's inventory / character sheet
  C              character sheet          Shift+1 - 4   that hero's sheet
  Drag a portrait onto another   swap their places in the formation
  Right-click    split a stack (one goes onto your cursor), lift an item out of a quick slot,
                 or cancel (a spell target, a "Raise whom?", a portrait drag; Esc does too)
  Shift+right-click   use one item from a stack

CASTING
  The Mage casts from a spellbook (Magic Missile, Shield, Fireball, Hold Monster); the Cleric
  from a holy symbol (Heal, Bless). Click the hand holding it and a spell list opens: each spell has its own
  icon and mana cost, the hero's mana is at the top, and spells you cannot afford are greyed.
  Pick a spell, then a hero if it needs one. The list always opens on top of everything and keeps
  clear of the map (below it for the top row of heroes).
  The symbol must be in a hand, not the backpack: "You need your holy symbol."
  When the Cleric casts Heal or Bless the symbol glows and a gold light sweeps the corridor.

LIGHT AND DARKNESS
  No light follows the party any more. Your eyes adjust enough to make out the nearest walls and
  a shape in the dark; the rest is the wall torches and whatever a hero holds. The Cleric starts
  with a lit torch and two candles in a quick slot.
  A torch or candle only shines in a hand, and it takes that hand: no weapon or holy symbol there.
  It burns only while held (a thin bar under the hand shows what is left; near the end the flame
  sputters), then "...'s torch gutters out." Put it away and it goes out, keeping its time.
  Torch: bright, about 4 minutes. Candle: dim and close, about 10 minutes. Click one in a quick
  slot to take it up; click again to swap back to what that hand held.
  A torch is a poor weapon with a little fire in it: slimes hate fire and back away from it.
  Fumble with a torch and it rolls onto the floor ahead, still burning. Pick it up again.

LOOT
  Right-click an item on the floor in front: a list of the heroes opens, with how much room each
  has in their backpack and who can't use it. Click a hero (or press 1 - 4) to put it straight
  in their pack. With several items on the tile, TAKE ALL shares them out. The dead can still
  carry (they are listed last). Esc or a click elsewhere closes it.

HOLD MONSTER
  The Mage binds the monster directly in front in cold blue runes for 5 seconds: it can't move
  or strike, and blows on it crit far more often (the Thief most of all). 5 mana, then 12 seconds
  before he can cast it again. Skeletons and slimes are held almost every time (slimes wobble free
  a little sooner); a skeleton guard shrugs it off now and then.

THE BEAR TRAP
  Two rusty bear traps lie in the dungeon. Click one in a hand or quick slot, or drop it from your
  cursor onto the empty tile in front, to set it there (it takes a moment; the Thief is quicker).
  Nothing gets past it: the first monster to step on it is snapped, hurt and held for 3 seconds.
  Then it lies closed on the floor to pick up and set again, unless the spring snaps. The party
  steps over its own traps; click an armed trap to take it back. Slimes ooze straight through.

THE PARTY TALKS
  The four heroes comment on deaths, revivals, empty mana, a good hit and a clumsy one, a held
  monster, a sprung trap, and the end of a fight. They keep quiet when it matters.

RAISING THE FALLEN
  Two Scrolls of Resurrection lie hidden on the floor. With one on your cursor, click a fallen
  hero's portrait (on the party panel, or the portrait strip in the inventory) to raise them at
  half health and no mana. From a quick slot: click the slot, then click the fallen hero.
  Right-click or Esc cancels. On a living hero it does nothing: "The scroll needs the fallen."
  A raised Cleric returns at full health but shaken: no mana and no spells for 20 seconds,
  shown by a dimmed holy symbol on the portrait.

FOOD
  Food heals slowly, potions at once. Click a hero's portrait with food on your cursor to feed
  them, or use it from a quick slot. Eating again replaces a meal still working. Mouldy bread is
  the green one: it heals a little and may make the eater sick for a few seconds.
  Some monsters leave food behind when they die.

CRITICAL HITS AND FAILS
  A landed hit can be a CRITICAL! for double damage (spells: half as much again). The Thief crits
  most, above all with a dagger; a hero's chance is on their character sheet. Monsters can crit
  too, rarely, but a crit won't kill a hero at half health or more.
  A miss can be a CRITICAL FAIL!: the hero stumbles and loses their next attack, or their weapon
  lands on the tile in front. Picking it up puts it back in their hand. Someone will comment.

HITS FROM BEHIND AND THE SIDES
  A monster behind you hits the back row (by default the Thief and the Mage); one at your side
  hits that side's column. The view lurches away from the blow, that edge of the screen glows
  red, and you hear it from that side. The first hit from behind in a fight says "Something's
  behind you!" Turn to face it: you can only strike forwards. Drag a portrait onto another to
  swap who stands where.

THE MAP
  The party sketches the dungeon as it goes: a small map on stained parchment sits in the top
  right of the 3D view (floors are an ink wash, walls thin ink lines, doors drawn as bars across
  the gap, red dot = locked, stairs / levers / found secrets as small symbols, a red arrow for
  where you face). The torchlight flickers faintly over it.
  M              full-screen map of the whole level, with a legend (M or Esc closes it)
  Tab            show / hide the small map        Shift+Tab   show / hide coordinates (debug)
  This demo has no fog of war yet, so the map shows the whole level around you.

ENEMY HEALTH BARS
  A small bar hangs over a monster once you hurt it, and over the one right in front of you;
  it fades away a few seconds after the last hit. The monster in front is also named in a strip
  at the top of the view. The lost chunk flashes, then drains. Options: ENEMY BARS ON / BOSS ONLY /
  OFF, for the old-school feel.

OTHER KEYS
  Esc            pause menu (Resume, Save, Load, Quit)
  F5 or K        save          L   load

OPTIONS (main menu)
  Master / Music / Effects volume, SCREEN (fullscreen or windowed), FOG on/off, HINTS on/off,
  PALETTE (snaps the 3D view to the game's 32 colours; applies when a game starts), ENEMY BARS
  (on, boss only, off), and FLASHING: REDUCED, which softens the flashes and stops all screen shake.
