# Open questions for the expansion design

Ten taste calls to settle before `docs/design/expansion_design.md` is written (phases 7d to 7l and 8b).
Each has every option and my pick. Answer with something like "1a, 2b, 3a", or "your picks".

**Answered 14 Nov 2026: "your picks", so option a for all ten.** The design is in
`docs/design/expansion_design.md`.

Written 14 Nov 2026. Not a question: the bear trap (7k.2) and torches and candles (7l.1) are already
built, so the design will describe them as they are and cover only what is left of those phases.

## 1. How scarce is light?

Floor 1 has 25 wall torches, all lit, so the dark hardly bites yet.

- **a (my pick):** only the first stretch stays lit; the rest become unlit sconces. A careful party
  finishes with light to spare, a careless one walks the last third by candle.
- **b:** mostly atmosphere; you never really run out.
- **c:** harsh; running out is likely on a first run.

## 2. Does fighting in the dark cost anything beyond not seeing?

- **a (my pick):** yes, a small one: heroes with no light are a bit less likely to hit. Monsters are
  not affected (they live there).
- **b:** no, darkness is only about what you can see.

## 3. Can an ambush kill a hero?

The roadmap says "hurt, never wipe".

- **a (my pick):** a healthy party never loses anyone, but a hero who walked in already badly hurt
  can die.
- **b:** nobody ever dies in an ambush; it only drains HP.

## 4. Who is the mini-boss at the stairs?

- **a (my pick):** the Cursed Warden from the parked `wip/boss-ambush` branch, moved from the key
  room to guard the stairs. The key room gets the giant hand instead.
- **b:** the Warden stays in the key room as designed, and something new guards the stairs.

## 5. Should a bear trap or Hold Monster work on the boss?

Traps are now set to "nothing gets past".

- **a (my pick):** yes, briefly: the trap holds him about 1.5 seconds, once. Hold Monster rarely
  lands. Clever play is rewarded without making him a pushover.
- **b:** the boss shrugs off both.

## 6. How far does levelling go on floor 1?

There are about 15 monsters and firewood for two or three camps, and levels only land at a campfire.

- **a (my pick):** most parties reach the stairs at level 3, thorough ones at 4. Level 5 waits for
  floor 2.
- **b:** level 5 is reachable on floor 1, as the roadmap says.

## 7. Is the campfire the only way to save?

- **a (my pick):** no. Camping saves too, but F5 and the pause menu keep working. Kinder for friends
  playing a demo.
- **b:** yes, camps are the only save points, which makes firewood matter much more.

## 8. Which legendaries go on floor 1?

The roadmap says two or three of the seven: Grudge, the Too Old For This Shield, the Gilded Hand, the
Tome of Screams, Penitent's Mace, the Rat King's Crown, the Unquenchable torch.

- **a (my pick):** Grudge (the talking sword, mid-floor, starts the arguments), Penitent's Mace
  (gives the Cleric something to complain about), and the Unquenchable torch as the reward right at
  the end, so it can't cancel the light economy.
- **b:** you name them.

## 9. Character generator: do the four heroes survive it?

All the banter is written for Brannoc, Brother Felix, Wren and Fennick.

- **a (my pick):** they stay as the ready-made party and the default. The generator lets you rename
  and re-roll them, voices follow the class, and exactly one of each class is required so the banter
  always works. No races for now.
- **b:** full Eye of the Beholder: races, any mix of classes. Banter then has to cope with two
  Fighters or no Cleric.

## 10. Bellius: does he show up on floor 1?

- **a (my pick):** one small trace, a note on a fallen adventurer, so the story has a seed.
- **b:** nothing yet; keep him for later.
